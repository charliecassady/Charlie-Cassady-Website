Computers Aren't Everything

The Apple Vision Pro and spatial computing have several benefits for the present and the future of society. This headset offers a variety of tools in the context of reality and computers and is beneficial to such fields as education, medicine, and the workforce. However, technology like this with such an abundant amount of tools, can overlook what genuine human behavior can create and do. Though the Apple Vision Pro can be put to good use in multiple aspects, it is not human and can stray away from human interaction and authenticity. People must be more conscious about what technology is meant to do versus what it is not meant to do. The Apple Vision Pro may have significantly interesting features, but it cannot create or sense emotions and does not have intrapersonal feelings . I believe that people tend to overestimate what technology can do, causing them to get attached and addicted to it, ultimately leading to less genuine social interactions and heavy reliance.

Computers and technology lack certain aspects that humans have. They are not as emotionally intelligent, do not have literal feelings, and cannot sense the user’s emotions. The Apple Vision Pro can be personalized to a certain extent, for example, one can personalize their education . This personalization will “foster a supportive learning environment where every student has the opportunity to thrive academically” (FXMedia Team). While utilizing the device in a personal way may work for education, it would not work if using it to understand feelings . Technology lacks that genuine human connection with an understanding of emotions. Feeling emotions is what makes us human, and translating it into technology is nearly impossible. According to the Salient Process, “it has no way of simulating or interpreting emotions. To understand emotion, AI needs to know how to interpret human experiences, not just intelligence. Feeling emotions is what makes us human, and translating this to a machine is likely impossible.” Trying to have devices understand what you are feeling would not succeed, but it could understand why you are feeling sad, happy, angry, etc. As another example, when I ask the internet why I am upset, it gives a very plain and automated response because it can not literally feel sadness. It can only understand what emotions are, not how they affect and feel towards others. After all, technology is made by humans, and emotional intelligence is not something one can necessarily describe. People need to realize this and understand that devices cannot replicate us fully, therefore we should not overuse them in certain aspects.

Not only does technology lack human intelligence and unique behaviors, but this causes harm to humans because technology is already so implemented into society, that it is hard to turn away from it, and it is easy to rely on it . In an experiment done by Stanford , they tested the psychological implications caused by using a mixed-reality headset - the Apple Vision Pro is considered a mixed-reality device. A critical piece that was found during this study was that people felt disconnected from others. The article states “users feeling socially disconnected from physically copresent others….one should not assume that the social presence of other people beamed in via passthrough is equivalent to face-to-face interaction” (Bailenson, et all). This backs up my point that technology can be harmful to human interactions at some point. Mixed reality can be so unimaginable, that it is unusual for us to understand. It is such a different experience, that it can almost feel unnatural for us to use it . Looking through a device to talk to someone, or to watch TV anywhere at any time is not and will never feel the same as talking face to face or watching TV in your living room. Utilizing these features too often could potentially harm social behavior and cause social skills to decrease the more technology like this is used. Not only did this study show that humans felt socially disconnected, but they also felt motion sickness, distortion with objects and movement, and frustration when using the headset . The article states “Subjects reported more simulator sickness, negative affect, and frustration when working in VR compared to a desktop computer” (Bailenson, et all). However, over a period of time, some people ended up adapting to these feelings and the use of the headset. But, in general, most people have these feelings when using it, which are clearly harmful. We should not be using devices over and over again if it makes us feel that way. Especially for aspects like entertainment, interactions, and work, because everyone can do that in person and in a more authentic and realistic way.

Society is very wrapped in technology and overuses it, ultimately leading people to believe technology can do everything . We use devices in everyday life-our phones, computers, television, etc.- as a main way of communication, work, gathering information, news, etc. Due to this, it can be easy to rely heavily on technology in our lives . However, there are multiple flaws that people do not recognize because it is such a popular aspect of life. A.I specifically does not have the answer to everything people might assume. It is made by us, there are bound to be flaws . Computers are not problem solving devices, they are a factual device that gives out answers theory already has programmed. For example, according to the article by Neuroscience News, “AI’s generation of text can propagate biases and even produce harmful, biased content without awareness.” There have been instances where technology produces false content and biased content. An article published by the Washington Post states “Artificial intelligence is automating the creation of fake news, spurring an explosion of web content mimicking factual articles that instead disseminates false information about elections, wars, and natural disasters.” It then proceeds to explain instances where AI and technology grasp information and it rapidly grows, but it is false information. This rapid growth causes more and more people to read it, and end up believing it. The amount of trust we put into random websites is harmful, but, everything is accurate on the internet, right? This is far from true.

Not enough people question the accuracy of information spread online, not enough people rely on their own knowledge, and not enough people understand that computers are not humans. Besides all of the features that may benefit certain aspects of society, if humans keep a constant reliance on technology, it will end up backfiring, whether that's individually or collectively in society. Technology will not be exactly like us, understand us fully, and experience life like us, so why do we rely so much and believe so much on the internet?
